Download the playable MoreRealistic.zip file directly:
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/quadural/MoreRealistic_FS25/main/MoreRealistic.zip

Place the ZIP unchanged into:
Documents\My Games\FarmingSimulator2025\mods\MoreRealistic.zip

Do not extract it. Do NOT use GitHub's "Code > Download ZIP": that
downloads the source repository, not a game-ready mod archive.
The playable ZIP must contain modDesc.xml at its root.
Many FS25 mods use the FS25_ prefix, but don't rename this archive to
FS25_MoreRealistic.zip yet: the existing MoreRealistic mod ID is
used by integrations such as Advanced Damage System.
